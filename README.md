# POWER-BI-PROJECT-MS-ECOMMERCE-DASHBOARD

**Executive Summary**

1.This project focuses on analyzing MS E-commerce sales data using Microsoft Power BI and Excel to derive actionable business insights.

2.The analysis provides a comprehensive overview of sales, profit, customer behavior, and product performance.

3.Interactive dashboards enable stakeholders to monitor KPIs and identify trends in real time.

4.The project highlights revenue drivers and profit leakages across categories and regions.

5.The final outcome supports data-driven decision-making to improve overall business performance.

**Business Problem**

1.The organization lacks a single, consolidated view of e-commerce sales data.

2.Sales and profit performance varies across products, regions, and time periods, but the reasons are unclear.

3.Difficulty in identifying high-margin vs. low-margin products impacts pricing strategies.

4.Limited understanding of customer purchasing patterns affects targeted marketing.

5.Absence of visual reporting makes it time-consuming for leadership to evaluate performance.

**Methodology**

1.Imported raw MS E-commerce sales data into Excel for initial exploration and cleaning.

2.Performed data transformation using Power Query to handle missing values and inconsistencies.

3.Built a structured data model and created DAX measures for sales, profit, growth rate, and KPIs.

4.Developed interactive Power BI dashboards with slicers, filters, and drill-through functionality.

5.Conducted trend, category, and regional analysis to generate actionable insights.

**Skills & Tools Used**

1.Microsoft Power BI – data modeling, DAX calculations, and dashboard design.

2.Microsoft Excel – data cleaning, pivot tables, and preprocessing.

3.Data Visualization – creating clear and impactful charts and reports.

4.Business Intelligence (BI) – KPI monitoring and performance analysis.

5.Analytical & Critical Thinking – interpreting data to solve business problems.

**Results**

1.Identified top-performing products and categories driving the majority of revenue.

2.Discovered seasonal trends and peak sales periods.

3.Highlighted regions with high sales but low profitability, indicating cost or discount issues.

4.Improved visibility into customer behavior and repeat purchase patterns.

5.Enabled faster and more accurate management decision-making through interactive dashboards.

**Next Steps / Recommendations**

1.Integrate real-time or more recent data to enhance decision-making.

2.Apply sales forecasting models to predict future demand.

3.Perform deeper customer segmentation for personalized marketing.

4.Analyze discount and pricing strategies to improve profit margins.

5.Expand the dashboard to include inventory and supply chain metrics.
